Matching transits

Probability that two events of this object have the same depth and shape, from their trapezoid fits. Events are never merged automatically: a different depth or shape only lowers the probability (other planets of the same star are expected). The duration of an incomplete transit is only a lower limit (≥), so its T14 term is one-sided.

Repeated-event candidates stale

Transit events of this object that look alike (depth, T14, ingress, and a joint refit of their light curves) and whose centre times share a period form a candidate family. Nothing is merged and no event is rejected: SAME / DIFFERENT only record your verdict on a pair (DIFFERENT keeps both events as separate signals, e.g. two planets). Families, their periods and the aliases that the star's own light curves rule out are recomputed at the next relphot db analyze; until then a family with a rejected member or a new verdict is marked stale. The T14 of an incomplete transit is only a lower limit (≥); periods are provisional (P = dt / k), and vetoed aliases stay listed.

Period verification

One row per re-observation (set of nights): the period found in the data against the literature period; delta = P_obs / harmonic - P_lit. A period longer than a night (1 d) is measured on tie-calibrated magnitudes without per-night offsets and needs a multi-night tie; phase coverage below 50% (in red) means the period is poorly sampled. LS-guided rows come from a re-run with your own period guess (see below).

Re-run (on demand)

Nothing is re-run unless you tick RERUN, fill an entry and press the run button. Per night tick EXOP (transit centre + width: trapezoid fit, adds a user event; search events are never touched) and/or VAR (period guess: adds an LS-guided estimate; PERIOD changes only if you adopt it). "All nights rerun" uses every night of the object; unticked, the period search uses only the chosen night. Clicking the light curve fills the transit centre of the entry you last edited. While RERUN is ticked, dragging horizontally on the light curve selects a span instead of zooming (the mode bar's zoom button brings zooming back): for an EXOP entry it fills the transit centre (the midpoint) and the width in hours, for a VAR-only entry the period guess (the span in days -- drag from one peak to the next), and it switches the entry to the night with data in the span. The worker (relphot db reprocess --watch) works the queue off.
